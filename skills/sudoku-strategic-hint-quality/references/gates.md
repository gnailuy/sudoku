# Strategic Hint Quality Gates

Consult this checklist while producing or auditing one strategy package. Canonical domain rules remain in the repository's hint, localization, and E2E documents.

## Blocking design gates

- One maintained real example is identified and reproducible on current default branches.
- The actual product board is the primary storyboard surface.
- The complete automatic candidate grid is visible from Step 1.
- The package preserves one exact board state, typed premises, target, and one atomic action.
- Each step adds one learner-visible fact or relation; no adjacent scene/text pair merely repeats.
- The causal bridge is complete before the action, and formal vocabulary appears only with the final application.
- Established evidence remains visible when later reasoning depends on it.
- Marks and links express typed logic, not decoration; color is never the only meaning carrier.
- The complete package is self-contained and stops for design approval before implementation.

## Blocking localization gates

- English, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese are authored from a locale-neutral intent brief.
- Simplified and Traditional Chinese are reviewed independently as complete sequences.
- A separate fresh-context reviewer can reject the copy and records concrete linguistic findings.
- No English-shaped subject order, mechanical one-to-one synonym substitution, stacked nominal phrase, redundant quantifier, or literal Sudoku metaphor survives.
- Shortening or regrouping preserves every condition, target, count, causal relation, and action.
- Catalog/ICU checks prove structure only; screenshots prove fit only; neither is claimed as naturalness evidence.
- A failed locale review reopens the whole affected sequence and blocks implementation.

## Blocking implementation gates

- Backend semantics, scenes, descriptors, and effect match the approved package exactly.
- Browser code renders typed data without inferring Sudoku logic or parsing English fallback.
- Every production catalog is complete with no fallback leakage.
- Focused regression, complete strategy matrices, terminal/browser nine-game journeys, responsive screenshots, repository quality gates, and CI pass.
- Documentation changes preserve one canonical owner and current-state-only Active prose.
- Evidence reports mechanical, semantic, editorial, visual, fresh-context, and CI results separately.

## Recurrent failure patterns

Reject or revise a package that shows any of these:

- generic “compare” or conclusion prose where the logical bridge is missing;
- a fixed step count that compresses complex logic or pads simple logic;
- a text-only or abstract storyboard when the real board is available;
- family batching used to claim an unreviewed individual strategy is complete;
- scope drift to deferred UI ideas or the next strategy before the current boundary closes;
- direct Chinese translation of English metaphors such as “sees”;
- coordinate-heavy Chinese that forces the reader to decode English clause order;
- a self-certified “independent rewrite” without independent evidence;
- screenshots presented as proof of linguistic quality;
- sparse design boards that omit the candidate grid shown by the real product;
- decorative arrows for subset, intersection, or fish confinement;
- implementation started before design approval; or
- a post-hoc documentation report used in place of the repository's pre-PR stop gate.
